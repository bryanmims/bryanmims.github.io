🏙️ Unit 5: Around Town

Grade 2 · Class 2C (E5 / E6)

Unit 5: Around Town & Transportation

SpeakingListeningVocabulary

Grade/Class: Grade 2, Class 2C (E5/E6)
Unit: Voices 2, Unit 5, pages 58-63 (Lessons 1-2, two class periods, ~40 min each)
Group Structure: Six teams: Red, Blue, Green, Purple, White, Black.
Prior Knowledge: Early elementary. Vocabulary and grammar are first exposure for most students; heavy visual support and short tasks throughout.

Content SWBAT (Lesson 1): 1. Identify and name places in town using target vocabulary. 2. Ask and answer "Is there a...?" questions about places in town.
Content SWBAT (Lesson 2): 3. Identify and name types of transportation using target vocabulary. 4. Ask and answer "Are there any...?" questions using plural nouns.

Language SWBAT (Lesson 1): Use "Is there a ___?" / "Yes, there is." / "No, there isn't."
Language SWBAT (Lesson 2): Use "Are there any ___?" / "Yes, there are." / "No, there aren't."

🎲 Extra Games & Activities (teacher reference)

🎯 Town Bingo

Grid of place icons. Teacher calls "Is there a...?" Students mark matching squares. First to fill a row/column wins.

🚦 Simon Says

TPR with places/transportation vocab. "Simon says drive like a truck!" "Simon says wait at the bus stop!"

🧠 Vehicle Memory Match

Card pairs face-down. Students flip two at a time to find matching vehicle pairs, naming each one aloud.

🏗️ Build-a-Town

Pairs draw 4 places on paper. Partners take turns asking "Is there a...?" / "Are there any...?" to guess what is on the map.

📐 Four Corners

Label 4 corners of the room with different places. Call out a place, students run to the matching corner.

💬 Is there a...? / Are there any...?

Is there a park on your street?
Yes, there is. / No, there isn't.
Frame: Is there a ___ on your street?
"a" + singular place → use "is / isn't". One thing, one question.
Are there any bus stops on your street?
Yes, there are. / No, there aren't.
Frame: Are there any ___s on your street?
"any" + plural place → use "are / aren't". Could be more than one!

🚗 Lesson 2: Transportation (pg 60-63)

🗣️ Warm-Up: Talk to Your Partner

How do you go to school? Car, bus, or bike?
학교에 어떻게 가나요? 자동차, 버스, 아니면 자전거?
Are there any buses near your house?
집 근처에 버스가 있나요?
What is the fastest way to travel?
가장 빠른 이동 방법은 무엇인가요?

🎯 Today's Goals

We will name 12 types of transportation.
우리는 12가지 교통수단의 이름을 말할 거예요.
We will ask "Are there any ___s in your town?"
우리는 'Are there any ___s in your town?'를 물어볼 거예요.
We will practice more with Is there / Are there.
우리는 Is there / Are there를 더 연습할 거예요.
Vocabulary: car, van, truck, motorcycle, train, bike, bus, taxi, subway, airplane, scooter, skateboard
Grammar: Are there any motorcycles/trucks in your town? Yes, there are. / No, there aren't.

💬 Are there any...?

Are there any parks in your town?
Yes, there are. / No, there aren't.
Frame: Are there any ___s in your town?
Review from Lesson 1: "any" + plural noun → are / aren't. Same pattern, new topic (transportation next).
Is there a toy store in Red Town?
Yes, there is.
Frame: Is there a ___ in Red Town?
Singular place, one question — matches the "Is there a...?" pattern from Lesson 1.
