Vocabulary — Chapter 10

Key Vocabulary

Click the Korean clue to reveal the English word. Each word appears in context from Ch.10.

propaganda (n) — misleading information spread to promote a cause or leader. Chiron reveals that the Titans' "Golden Age" was really a time of fear for mortals, not the peaceful myth Kronos wanted remembered.
drachma (n) — the golden coin used by gods and demigods. Percy is outfitted with drachmas alongside ordinary mortal money before he leaves camp.
celestial (adj) — belonging to the heavens or the divine world. Riptide is made of celestial bronze, which is why it can wound monsters but never harm an ordinary mortal.
unsheathe (v) — to pull a blade out of its case. The instant Percy uncaps the ballpoint pen Chiron hands him, it unsheathes into a full bronze sword.
chauffeur (v/n) — to drive someone as a job, or the driver who does it. Argus, the many-eyed head of camp security, chauffeurs Percy, Annabeth, and Grover to the bus depot.
disintegrate (v) — to break apart into dust or nothing. When Percy's celestial bronze blade connects, the monster doesn't bleed — it disintegrates completely.
grudgingly (adv) — done reluctantly, without wanting to. Percy accepts that flying is too dangerous for a son of Poseidon and grudgingly gives up the gift Luke worked hard to get him.
wail (v) — to cry out loudly in pain or grief. The chapter ends with the eerie sound of Mrs. Dodds wailing as the bus is struck.
Themes — Chapter 10

Theme Hub

Strongest themes through Ch.10

Each button hides the theme — reveal it, then read the three strongest evidence points including new ones from Ch.10.

Identity — Percy is becoming who he was always meant to be.
  • ❓ Percy started the book as "undetermined," unsure of his own power.
  • ⚔️ In Ch.10, Chiron entrusts him with Riptide — a weapon literally forged for a son of Poseidon. His identity is no longer a question mark; it is armed and visible.
  • 🛡️ Percy now carries physical proof of who he is everywhere he goes, whether or not anyone else can see it.
Family and sacrifice — Love often looks like something else from the outside.
  • 😔 Percy has always resented his mother for staying with Smelly Gabe.
  • 💡 Grover reveals that Sally married Gabe on purpose — his overpowering stench masks Percy's demigod scent from monsters. What looked like a bad choice was a sacrifice.
  • 🎁 Luke's shoes and Chiron's sword are also gifts driven by care — from people who are not Percy's family by blood, but act like family anyway.
Fate and destiny — Something keeps testing Percy before he even chooses his path.
  • 🚌 The Furies attack almost the moment the quest begins — before Percy has even left New York.
  • ⚡ Lightning strikes the bus the instant Percy raises his sword outdoors — as if some greater force is watching and reacting immediately.
  • 🧵 The pattern echoes the three Fates from earlier in the story — old, watching women who seem to know more than they say.
Power with direction — Percy is moving from reacting to choosing.
  • 💥 Earlier, Percy's power activated on its own — the fountain, the bathroom pipes.
  • 🗡️ In Ch.10, Percy consciously grabs the wheel, pulls the brake, and draws Riptide — all deliberate choices under pressure, not accidents.
  • 🤝 He fights alongside Annabeth and Grover as a team, not alone — his power now works together with a plan.

📖 Evidence from the text
  • 🎁 Luke gives Percy magic winged shoes as a going-away gift, even though the two boys could be seen as rivals.
  • ⚔️ Chiron hands over Riptide — a sword that has clearly been kept safe and waiting for exactly this moment.
  • 👟 Percy immediately regifts the shoes to Grover once he realizes flying is too dangerous for him.
Gifts as trust and care — Every object Percy receives before the quest is really a relationship made physical. People show they care through what they give, not just what they say.
📖 Evidence from the text
  • 🎒 For the first time, Percy is equipped and prepared instead of caught by surprise.
  • 🗡️ He now carries a real weapon and knows, at least in theory, how to use it.
  • 🚪 Leaving Thalia's hill behind means leaving the safety of camp completely — there is no adult supervision on this trip.
Coming of age — Being handed real tools and real responsibility marks the moment Percy stops being protected and starts protecting others.
📖 Evidence from the text
  • ⚡ Annabeth tells Percy that Athena and Poseidon don't get along.
  • 😤 The comment causes real tension between Percy and Annabeth in the car, even though neither of them chose their parent.
  • 🧬 Their argument mirrors old family conflicts neither of them started.
Inherited conflict — Family rivalries can pass down to the next generation even when the children want nothing to do with them.
📖 Evidence from the text
  • 😱 The bus lurches out of control the moment the Furies attack, and passengers panic.
  • 🫥 Percy uses Annabeth's invisibility cap to buy time instead of fighting immediately.
  • 🧠 He thinks fast under pressure — grabbing the wheel, then the brake — rather than freezing.
Fear and survival — Real danger reveals what a person actually does under pressure, not just what they say they would do.
Summary — Chapter 10

Chapter Summary

Click to expand the bullet-point summary.

  • 📜 Percy asks Chiron what the world was like before the gods. Chiron explains the Titans' "Golden Age" was really propaganda — Kronos cared nothing for humans, and life only improved once Zeus and the Olympians took over.
  • 😤 On the car ride to the bus, Percy and Annabeth argue after she reveals that Athena and Poseidon don't get along.
  • 💡 Grover tells Percy the real reason his mother married Smelly Gabe: Gabe's overpowering smell masks Percy's demigod scent from monsters. It was protection disguised as a bad choice.
  • 🎒 The camp outfits the trio for the road: mortal money and drachmas, ambrosia and nectar, Annabeth's invisibility cap and bronze knife, Grover's reed pipes and snacks.
  • 👁️ At Thalia's hill, Argus — the many-eyed head of camp security — arrives to drive them to the bus depot in Manhattan.
  • 👟 Luke gives Percy a pair of magic winged sneakers, a gift from Hermes. Touched, Percy passes them to Grover once Chiron confirms that flying puts him in Zeus's territory.
  • ⚔️ Chiron gives Percy his own pen — Anaklusmos, or Riptide — which transforms into a celestial bronze sword. It can't hurt mortals, can't be lost, and will always return to Percy's pocket.
  • 🚌 In the city, the three board a Greyhound bus. Mrs. Dodds boards too, along with two identical sisters — the Furies.
  • 🫥 The Furies announce a restroom break to get to the back of the bus. Annabeth gives Percy her invisibility cap, and he sneaks toward the front — but the Furies attack Annabeth and Grover instead.
  • 🚨 Still invisible, Percy grabs the steering wheel and then the emergency brake, sending the bus off the road and into the New Jersey woods.
  • ⚔️ Percy uncaps Riptide and fights alongside his friends. They tie up Mrs. Dodds and destroy one of her sisters.
  • ⚡ Lightning strikes the bus, and the trio hears Mrs. Dodds wailing from inside as they flee into the woods — losing all their luggage and supplies in the process.

What Ch.10 adds to Percy's arc
  • ⚔️ He receives Riptide — the first weapon truly his own, entrusted to him by Chiron personally.
  • 🧠 On the bus, Percy makes fast, deliberate choices (the wheel, the brake, the sword) instead of reacting on instinct alone.
  • 😔 Learning the truth about Gabe forces Percy to reconsider a resentment he has carried for years.
  • 🤝 He fights as part of a team for the first time on the road, not alone.
🧠 Character Development Questions
  • ⚔️ How is Percy's fight on the bus different from his earlier moments of power (the fountain, the bathroom)? What does the difference show about his growth?
  • 💡 Percy has resented his mother's choice to stay with Gabe for years. How might learning the real reason change how he sees his whole childhood?
  • 🤝 Why might fighting alongside Annabeth and Grover — instead of alone — matter for Percy's development as a hero?
What Ch.10 adds to Annabeth's arc
  • ⚡ She tells Percy directly that Athena and Poseidon don't get along — she doesn't soften it, even knowing it might cause tension.
  • 🎒 She packs practically and strategically: a weapon, a magic item, and a book — she is always preparing.
  • 🫥 She gives up her invisibility cap to protect Percy without hesitation when the Furies attack.
🧠 Character Development Questions
  • ⚡ Why might Annabeth choose to be blunt about the Athena–Poseidon rivalry instead of avoiding the topic?
  • 🫥 Annabeth hands over her only magic item to protect Percy. What does that choice reveal about her priorities?
What Ch.10 adds to Grover's arc
  • 💡 Grover is the one who finally tells Percy the truth about why his mother stayed with Gabe — showing he understands Percy better than Percy understands himself sometimes.
  • 👟 He receives Percy's winged shoes and is thrilled, even though he immediately struggles to control them.
  • 😨 He is caught in the direct attack from the Furies alongside Annabeth, showing he is now genuinely in danger, not just a nervous bystander.
🧠 Character Development Questions
  • 💡 Why do you think Grover — rather than Percy's mother herself — is the one who finally explains the truth about Gabe?
  • 👟 What might it mean for Grover, specifically, to be the one who ends up with the winged shoes?

Discussion — Chapter 10

Discussion Questions

8 questions. Click 🇰🇷 to read in Korean. Click 💡 to see a strong model answer.

💬 Personal & Analytical
1. Chiron reveals that the Titans' "Golden Age" was really propaganda — a comfortable story that hid a harsher truth. Can you think of a time you learned that a comfortable story you believed wasn't quite true?
카이론은 타이탄의 "황금시대"가 사실 선전이었다고 밝힙니다 — 더 가혹한 진실을 감춘 편안한 이야기였죠. 여러분이 믿었던 편안한 이야기가 사실이 아니었다는 것을 알게 된 적이 있나요?
A strong answer identifies the pattern Riordan is pointing to: those in power often control the story told about their own rule, and "golden ages" are usually golden only for the people telling the story. Chiron's correction shows that history depends on who is allowed to speak. Connecting personally, students might mention a family story, a school legend, or a historical event they later learned was oversimplified. The strongest answers explain not just that the story changed, but how learning the truth changed their feelings about it.
2. Grover reveals that Sally married Gabe on purpose to protect Percy — a sacrifice that looked, from the outside, like a bad decision. Why is it sometimes hard to recognize sacrifice when we're the one being protected?
그로버는 샐리가 퍼시를 보호하기 위해 일부러 게이브와 결혼했다는 것을 밝힙니다 — 겉보기엔 나쁜 결정처럼 보였던 희생이었죠. 우리가 보호받는 당사자일 때 왜 희생을 알아차리기 어려운 걸까요?
It's difficult because sacrifice, by definition, is often invisible to the person it protects — the whole point is that the protected person doesn't have to see the cost. Percy only experienced Gabe's rudeness and smell; he had no way of knowing the smell was doing a job. A strong answer notes that this creates a painful irony: the people who sacrifice the most for us are often the ones we resent the most, precisely because we can't see what we're being spared from. Recognizing sacrifice usually requires distance, information, or time — exactly what Percy lacked until Grover explained it.
3. Percy passes the winged shoes to Grover instead of using them himself, even though it was a personal gift from Luke. What does this choice show about how Percy is starting to think as a leader?
퍼시는 루크가 자신에게 준 개인적인 선물임에도 불구하고 날개 달린 신발을 직접 사용하는 대신 그로버에게 줍니다. 이 선택은 퍼시가 리더로서 어떻게 생각하기 시작했는지 무엇을 보여주나요?
Giving away a meaningful personal gift for practical safety reasons shows Percy prioritizing the mission and the team's overall safety over his own feelings or pride. A less mature character might have kept the shoes out of attachment to Luke's gesture, or worn them despite the risk. Instead, Percy makes a clear-eyed judgment: he can't safely use them, but Grover can benefit from them. A strong answer connects this small moment to leadership — leaders often have to set aside what feels good personally in favor of what serves the group.
4. The Furies attack almost immediately once the quest begins, before Percy has fully left the city. Why might Riordan choose to start the danger this early instead of giving Percy a calm start to the journey?
퀘스트가 시작되자마자 복수의 여신들이 거의 즉시 공격합니다. 퍼시가 아직 도시를 완전히 떠나기도 전이죠. 리오단은 왜 여정에 평온한 시작을 주는 대신 이렇게 일찍 위험을 시작하려고 할까요?
Starting the danger immediately raises the stakes and signals to the reader that this quest will not follow a gentle, predictable pace — it establishes urgency right away. It also forces Percy to use his new gifts (Riptide, the cap) almost instantly, which lets the reader see them in action rather than just hearing about them. A strong answer identifies this as a pacing choice: early conflict hooks the reader and proves that camp's warnings about real danger were not exaggerated.
🧠 Character Development
5. Compare Percy's power in Ch.10 (grabbing the wheel, drawing Riptide) to his earlier power surges (the fountain, the bathroom pipes). What has changed about how he uses it?
Ch.10에서 퍼시의 힘(운전대를 잡고, 리프타이드를 뽑는 것)을 이전의 힘의 폭발(분수, 화장실 배관)과 비교해보세요. 그가 힘을 사용하는 방식에서 무엇이 달라졌나요?
⚡ Earlier, Percy's power activated involuntarily — the water responded to his fear or anger without his permission. 🧠 In Ch.10, by contrast, Percy makes a series of conscious decisions under extreme pressure: grabbing the wheel, pulling the brake, drawing his sword. 🗡️ This is not power acting on him — it's Percy acting with intention, even though he's scared. A strong answer names this shift clearly: from reactive and accidental to deliberate and chosen, and connects it to what kind of hero Percy is becoming — one who learns to act instead of just react.
6. Annabeth tells Percy bluntly that Athena and Poseidon don't get along, even though it causes tension between them. What does her directness reveal about her values?
아나베스는 그것이 둘 사이에 긴장을 유발함에도 불구하고 아테나와 포세이돈이 사이가 좋지 않다고 퍼시에게 솔직하게 말합니다. 그녀의 솔직함은 그녀의 가치관에 대해 무엇을 드러내나요?
Annabeth values honesty over comfort — she would rather Percy know an uncomfortable truth upfront than let him find out later and feel blindsided. This is consistent with her strategic, planning-focused personality from earlier chapters: she prefers clear information to smooth but misleading relationships. A strong answer notes this is a form of respect, even if it doesn't feel kind in the moment — she is treating Percy as someone capable of handling difficult truths, not someone to be managed or protected from reality.
7. Grover is the one who finally explains why Sally married Gabe — not Percy's mother herself. Why might Riordan have chosen Grover, specifically, to deliver this information?
샐리가 왜 게이브와 결혼했는지 마침내 설명하는 사람은 퍼시의 어머니 자신이 아니라 그로버입니다. 리오단은 왜 하필 그로버를 선택해서 이 정보를 전달하게 했을까요?
Grover has protected Percy from the shadows for years and often understands emotional truths that Percy himself hasn't processed — this fits his established role as quiet observer and protector. It also may say something about Sally: perhaps she was never able to explain it to Percy herself, whether out of guilt, protectiveness, or simply not wanting her son to carry that knowledge as a child. A strong answer considers both possibilities — what it says about Grover's role, and what it might say about the limits of what a parent can say directly to their child.
8. By the end of Ch.10, Percy has lost all his luggage, fought real monsters away from camp, and used Riptide for the first time in a true battle. How has the "safety net" around Percy changed since Ch.1, and what does that mean for the rest of the quest?
Ch.10이 끝날 무렵 퍼시는 모든 짐을 잃었고, 캠프를 벗어나 진짜 괴물들과 싸웠으며, 진짜 전투에서 처음으로 리프타이드를 사용했습니다. Ch.1 이후로 퍼시 주변의 "안전망"은 어떻게 변했나요? 그리고 이것이 남은 퀘스트에 어떤 의미를 가질까요?
In Ch.1, Percy had teachers, his mother, and eventually Chiron and camp watching over him — even his most dangerous moments happened inside systems built to protect him. By the end of Ch.10, all of that structure is gone: no camp, no adults, no supplies, and no guaranteed safety. A strong answer notes that this stripped-down situation forces real growth — Percy can no longer rely on rescue, so every choice he makes now carries full weight. This sets up the rest of the quest as a genuine test of what he's actually capable of, not just what he can do with backup nearby.
